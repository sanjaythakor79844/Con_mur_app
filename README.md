# Aaha Health Companion

Design a complete production-ready mobile application UI/UX in Figma for **Aaha Companion App**.

Aaha Companion App is the patient-facing companion to the **Aaha Health Kiosk**. It helps users continue their healthcare journey after completing a health screening. Users can view their screening results, upload laboratory reports, receive personalised health explanations from Aaha, book consultations, discover Aaha Health Centres, access therapies and wellness services, and manage their long-term health journey.

IMPORTANT BRAND RULES

• Never use the words "AI Assistant", "AI Chatbot", "AI Health Assistant", or "Artificial Intelligence" anywhere in the UI.

• Always refer to the virtual healthcare guide as **Aaha**.

• Aaha is the friendly healthcare companion and voice of the platform.

• Aaha explains reports, guides users, answers health-related questions, and helps them continue their care journey.

• Keep the experience warm, trustworthy, human-centred, premium and medical.

• The application should naturally encourage users to visit an Aaha Health Centre without looking like an advertisement.
